Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

Information we process

Builder processes account information, project content, usage information and AI-provider configuration needed to operate the service.

AI provider keys

Provider credentials entered in Builder are sent to authenticated server-side functions. They are encrypted before storage and are not placed in browser local storage or returned to the browser in full.

Project data

Your projects are tenant-scoped. Database policies restrict records to the signed-in user or workspace membership rules.

Analytics

Optional analytics is disabled until you provide an analytics measurement ID and the visitor gives consent. Essential authentication storage may still be used to keep the service signed in.

Payments

Payment details are handled by the configured payment processor. Builder should not store card numbers, CVV codes or equivalent payment credentials.

Contact

Before launch, replace the configured contact address in CONTACT_ADDRESS with your real business/contact address. Do not publish an invented address.
