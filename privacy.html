Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

Information we process

Project X processes account information, user intentions, project content, usage information, and AI-provider configuration needed to operate the service.

AI provider keys & Security

Provider credentials entered in Project X are sent to authenticated server-side functions. They are encrypted before storage and are not placed in browser local storage or returned to the browser in full.

Project data & Tenant Isolation

Your projects and intentions are tenant-scoped. Database security policies restrict records to the signed-in user or workspace membership rules.

Analytics

Optional analytics is disabled until you provide an analytics measurement ID and the visitor gives consent. Essential authentication storage may still be used to keep the service signed in.

Payments

Payment details are handled by the configured payment processor. Project X does not store raw credit card numbers or sensitive payment keys on the client.

Contact

For questions regarding this policy, reach out to the Project X support team.
